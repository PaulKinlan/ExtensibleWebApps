Lisbon, 12 to 16 May

Four days of trams, tiles and custard tarts.

Wednesday 12 · Arrive

  • Land 11:40, metro red line to Alameda, then green to Baixa-Chiado
  • Check in at Rua das Flores 28
  • Sunset at Miradouro de Santa Catarina

Thursday 13 · Alfama

  • Tram 28 from Martim Moniz, early, before the queue
  • Castelo de São Jorge
  • Fado at Tasca do Chico, book ahead

Friday 14 · Belém

  • Pastéis de Belém, then the Jerónimos Monastery
  • Walk the river to the MAAT

Saturday 15 · Sintra

  • Train from Rossio, 40 minutes
  • Pena Palace and the Moorish Castle

Flight home Sunday 16 at 17:05. Hotel: +351 21 000 0000.
