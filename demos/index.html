Each of these is an app made of plugins: one JavaScript file each, run in its own worker. Open one, then use Customize (bottom left) to see what it's made of and add your own, or any of the site's plugins. Every app here keeps its data in this browser, and they share it: the calendar shows the contacts app's birthdays.
