Each of these is an app made of plugins: one JavaScript file each, run in its own worker. Open one, then use Customize (bottom left) to see what it's made of and add your own. Every app here keeps its data in this browser, and they share it: the calendar shows the contacts app's birthdays.

Showcase apps

Apps built to see how far the platform's UI goes: rich layouts and plugins that add to each other's views, still drawn from plain HTML templates with no style attributes.

  • Studio board

    A project board with covers, labels and people. Due dates, checklists and limits are separate plugins that add to the board's cards and columns, and each card has its own screen.

    Shows: slots in a view, transforms, screens, commands, a theme.

  • Surveys

    Build a survey and share a link anyone can answer. Open it in a space and the space's server serves the form, keeps the answers and emails a daily digest, with no one's browser open.

    Shows: a server plugin, public routes, a schedule, email.

Apps with slots for plugins

  • Reading list

    Books to read, and when they're due back. One plugin owns the books, another lists the ones due soon, and an importer asks before it adds any.

    Shows: collections, review, screens with their own address, sharing.

  • Habits

    Habits to keep, ticked off each day. The app the developer guide builds, one step at a time.

    Shows: a first plugin, live views.

  • Spending

    What you spent, against a budget, by category. Undo takes back any change, and a spreadsheet can come in or go out.

    Shows: plugins reading one collection, undo, import and export.

  • Recipes

    Recipes that scale, and a shopping list filled from them.

    Shows: two collections, views drawn in code.

  • Contacts

    People, with a live search and their birthdays. A vCard importer asks before it adds anyone.

    Shows: live search, review.

  • Household

    A shopping list and the chores, shared by everyone in the house. Open it in a space and each phone keeps a copy, works offline, and says who added, got or did what.

    Shows: a space shared by several people, sync, offline edits, drafts when two people change the same thing.

  • Calendar

    A month grid, and an agenda that includes birthdays from the contacts app.

    Shows: nested templates, reading another app's data.

Pages with no slots

Ordinary pages whose author left no room for plugins. Customize can still add to them, and the author decides which parts are open, reviewed or locked.

  • Trip

    A plain itinerary. Add a countdown after its heading, and a packing list to its footer.

    Shows: places.

  • Menu

    A café menu. Add a plugin that changes every price's currency, or hides the newsletter box.

    Shows: replacing and hiding parts of a page.

  • Invoice

    An invoice with parts marked for review, and one locked until you unlock it.

    Shows: the author's open, review and locked marks.

Agents

  • Librarian

    The reading list, with two AI agents that fill in each new book's details, through review, and ask you when they aren't sure.

    Shows: agents, the board. Needs a model key.

  • Security

    The librarian's agents, with a third that checks books and tool calls first, and blocks what looks like a card number.

    Shows: an agent guarding others. Needs a model key.

  • Assistant

    The reading list with the standard assistant and builder agents, and a chat to write to them. The assistant uses the app's own commands; the builder writes a plugin from what you describe.

    Shows: standard agents, acting for you. Needs a model key.
